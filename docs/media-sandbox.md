# Android 15 media shared memory

The `618fa11930c9` five-minute scrcpy workload retains eight mediaextractor aborts at `memfd_create`, followed by a Settings `SeekBarVolumizer`/Ringtone preparation crash. Its native trace runs through libcutils' memfd probe, `ashmem_create_region`, `MemoryDealer` and `RemoteDataSource`. Successful video decoding does not qualify this failed workload.

Android 15 royd selects libcutils' sealed-memfd backend. The extractor's stock x86_64 policy omits creation and sizing of those regions. The probe requires `MFD_CLOEXEC | MFD_NOEXEC_SEAL`, `F_ADD_SEALS(F_SEAL_FUTURE_WRITE)` and close. Allocation additionally sizes the region and seals growth/shrinkage; read-only protection reads seals and adds future-write/seal protection. Media buffer sizes vary. Kernel descriptor permissions, seals and the existing process memory limit remain independent controls.

Patch `0069` selects a required policy addition only on x86_64 with `ROYD_CONTAINER=1`, a SELinux enforcement-interface probe failing with exactly `ENOENT`, and both `ro.treble.enabled` and `sys.use_memfd` enabled. Other cases retain the original call. Keep the stock base and optional vendor policy, then append the required addition in memory. Missing or unreadable stock/required files remain fatal. Existing two-argument/list loader interfaces and optional-vendor failures retain their behaviour. Do not wrap the stock policy in another include: Minijail rejects the resulting nested crash-dump includes. Reading the current stock file also preserves subsequent APEX policy updates.

The addition permits only memfd flags `0x9`, non-negative x86_64 `ftruncate` lengths, `F_GET_SEALS` and the three libcutils `F_ADD_SEALS` combinations. It does not grant executable/HugeTLB memfd flags, other seal commands or general descriptor control. Minijail's existing diagnostic logging mode can independently allow `fcntl`; this change does not remove or narrow that stock allowance, and does not claim complete descriptor confinement. No descriptor provenance check follows from a syscall argument filter. The service still enters minijail with no-new-privileges and seccomp before publishing Binder.

`make android15-mediaextractor-memfd-container-test` exercises the actual gate. With `ROYD_TEST_MEDIA_TREE` pointing to the applied Android 15 checkout, it also compiles pinned Minijail, executes real kernel filters in disposable children and checks the applied loader's required-file, permission and pipe failures. Both logging modes must preserve the full stock policy and required shared-memory sequence. Negative probes trap within their child and exit without requesting host core dumps.

`make runtime-mediaextractor-test` uses a fresh labelled container and data volume. A zero-permission ordinary test APK prepares its own uncompressed 250 ms WAV asset five times and records real duration/audio-track observations. Repeated Sound Settings launches must preserve Settings and extractor PIDs. Require active seccomp/no-new-privileges, the bounded policy marker, unchanged Docker/SystemServer identity and full independent pre/post runtime assertions, including after workload failure. Keep a new `ROYD_MEDIA_OUTPUT_DIR` for every attempt, with the APK/fixture, observations, service status, UI captures, full log/inspect and format-1 manifest. This does not qualify audible host output, arbitrary codecs, complete media compatibility or other Android/architecture profiles.

Rebuilt-image runtime and fresh scrcpy qualification remain required. Earlier failed evidence stays failed; focused contracts alone do not establish support.
